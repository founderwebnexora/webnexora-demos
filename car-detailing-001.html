Services / 04

A considered approach to every surface.

Each commission is scoped individually. We begin with a 40-point inspection and a written protocol — no two vehicles leave the studio the same way.

01 / Signature

Paint correction & ceramic architecture

Multi-stage machine polishing followed by a 9H silica ceramic matrix. Finished under controlled lighting to remove 95% of defect depth.

02 / Protection

Stealth PPF

Self-healing urethane film, knifeless installed. Invisible where it matters, structural where it counts.

A documented protocol for every vehicle.

  • Pre-wash decontamination & iron dissolution

  • Dual-action & rotary correction stages
  • IPA panel wipe & ceramic curing chamber

03 — How We Build It

Four steps.
One obsession.

Every burger at Blaze follows the same ritual — a process refined across 120,000 orders and counting.

Step 01

Hand-formed,
never frozen.

100% chuck-brisket blend, ground in-house every morning. Shaped to order, never pre-made.

Step 02

Smashed
at 500°F.

Cast-iron plancha, 10 seconds of pressure. That's how you get the crust.

Step 03

Sauces,
in-house.

Seven signature sauces. Made weekly. No bottles.

Step 04

Brioche, toasted in beef tallow.

That's the secret most places won't tell you. Golden, crisp, and just a little bit dangerous.
